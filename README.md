## SPELLS & SORCERERS
Spells & Sorcerers ('sas' for short) is a magic mod with **highly** customizable staffs.

You can find mysterious obelisks scattered around in long gone civilizations' ruins! Where might they take you?  
Discover their writings on the magic's possibilities and limits!

Trick your enemies' minds into doing your bidding! Bend their will into praying to you as their lord! Hide yourself from others!
